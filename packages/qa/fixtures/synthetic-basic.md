# Introduction to Data Literacy

## Course goal
Students will interpret simple tables and charts and explain evidence-based conclusions.

## Learning objectives
- Identify variables and data types.
- Read bar charts and line charts accurately.
- Calculate and interpret a simple average.
- Explain one limitation of a dataset.

## Structure
### Week 1 — Data and variables
Introduce categorical and numerical variables.

### Week 2 — Reading charts
Practice bar charts and line charts.

## Assessment
Create one short assignment asking students to interpret a chart and justify a conclusion.
Create one quiz containing at least one multiple-choice, true/false, short-answer, and essay question.
